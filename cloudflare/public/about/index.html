About Rian Cullet

Rian Cullet is a generational business based in Delhi. Established in 1996, it brings 65+ years of combined experience in glass cullet, built across generations.

We specialise in the collection, sorting, processing and recycling of foreign and post-consumer glass cullets, making them suitable for reuse in glass manufacturing. Through systematic sorting and processing, we help convert waste glass into a valuable raw material that can be fed into glass furnaces.

Over the years, Rian Cullet has served 35+ companies across 22+ states, pan-India, building trust within the glass manufacturing and recycling ecosystem.

Our work also contributes to reducing the amount of glass waste going to landfills, while creating an economically valuable source of recycled material.

Our story

A vision that began in 1996.

The journey of Rian Cullet is driven by the vision and leadership of its founder, Ms. Indu Bhatia. With a strong focus on sustainability and responsible waste management, she established the foundation for a business dedicated to transforming waste glass into a valuable and reusable resource.

Her vision has been to create a reliable source of quality glass cullet for the glass manufacturing industry while contributing towards a cleaner and more sustainable environment.
