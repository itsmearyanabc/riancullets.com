Process

From discarded glass to industrial resource

Collection, sorting, processing and supply. Four stages that turn recovered glass into a material a furnace can accept.

Systematic sorting and processing

Recovered glass arrives mixed. What makes it usable again is the discipline applied to it: separation by type and by colour, then preparation to a condition a glass furnace can accept.

Through systematic sorting and processing, we help convert waste glass into a valuable raw material that can be fed into glass furnaces.

Sustainability

Building a more circular glass industry

Glass that is recovered, sorted and processed can re-enter manufacturing as raw material. The cycle closes rather than ending at landfill.

  1. Waste glass Glass that has served its first purpose.
  2. Recovery Collected from relevant streams.
  3. Sorting Separated by type and colour.
  4. Processing Prepared into usable cullet.
  5. Cullet Ready for the furnace.
  6. New glass Returned to manufacturing.
