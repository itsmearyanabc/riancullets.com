Sustainability

Building a more circular glass industry

Glass recovered, sorted and processed can re-enter manufacturing as raw material rather than ending at landfill.

Our objective

At Rian Cullet, our objective is to ensure that waste glass is properly sorted, processed, recycled and returned to the manufacturing cycle, helping create a more sustainable and circular glass industry.

Our work also contributes to reducing the amount of glass waste going to landfills, while creating an economically valuable source of recycled material.

Glass is well suited to this. It can be recovered, sorted by colour and returned to the furnace as an input rather than treated solely as waste.

Why recycled glass matters

Lower melting requirements

02

Energy efficiency

Using recycled glass as a raw material can contribute to lower energy requirements, and to the emissions associated with them.
