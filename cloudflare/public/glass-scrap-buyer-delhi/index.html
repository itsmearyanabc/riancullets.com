Sell your glass scrap in Delhi NCR

We collect broken glass, waste bottles and factory glass waste, and return it to the manufacturing cycle as cullet.

Every glass bottle, jar and broken pane is raw material for a glass furnace. Rian Cullet collects that glass across Delhi NCR, sorts it by type and colour, and processes it into cullet that glass manufacturers melt down into new glass.

We have done this from Tikri Kalan, Delhi, since 1996. If your business generates glass waste regularly, or you aggregate it from others, talk to us.

01

Glass we collect

  • Waste glass bottles: beer, liquor, wine, soft drink, sauce and pharmaceutical bottles.
  • Glass jars and other container glass.
  • Broken container glass, in clear, amber or green.
  • Factory glass waste: rejects, breakage and process cullet from glass plants and bottling lines.
  • Flat glass waste, such as sheet glass offcuts, kept separate from container glass.
02

Glass that usually cannot be recycled with bottles

These melt at different temperatures or carry coatings and contaminants that spoil a furnace batch. Ask us before sending them.

  • Heat-resistant (borosilicate) cookware and laboratory glass.
  • Ceramics, porcelain, crockery and stone mixed in with glass.
  • Light bulbs, tube lights and CFLs.
  • Mirrors, and laminated or wired safety glass.
  • Old television and monitor screens (CRT glass).
03

Who we work with

  • Glass manufacturing plants with in-house breakage and rejects.
  • Breweries, distilleries, bottlers and beverage companies.
  • Hotels, restaurants, bars and caterers.
  • Scrap dealers, kabadiwalas and waste aggregators.
  • Material recovery facilities and waste management operators.
04

How it works

  1. Send an enquiry with the type of glass, its colour, the approximate quantity and where it is.
  2. We confirm whether we can take it and agree collection or delivery to our Tikri Kalan yard.
  3. The glass is checked, sorted by type and colour, and processed into cullet.
  4. That cullet is supplied to glass manufacturers and melted into new glass.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you accept broken glass?

Yes. Cullet is broken glass by definition, so breakage is not a problem. What matters is that it is glass of the right kind, and as free as possible of ceramics, stone, metal and rubbish.

Do I need to separate glass by colour?

Sorting by colour is part of our work, so mixed loads can be discussed. Glass that is already separated into clear, amber and green is easier to process and is generally more valuable.

Can you collect from outside Delhi?

Our yard is in Delhi and most collection is across Delhi NCR. For regular volumes elsewhere, send an enquiry and we will tell you what is practical.

Do you take light bulbs or tube lights?

No. Lamps contain metals, coatings and in some cases mercury, and are handled through e-waste channels, not glass cullet.

Is there a minimum quantity?

It depends on the material and the location. Tell us what you have and how often it is generated, and we will advise.
