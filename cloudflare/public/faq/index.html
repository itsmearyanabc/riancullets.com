Questions

Frequently asked

What is glass cullet?

Glass cullet is recycled or broken glass that has been processed and prepared for melting and reuse in the production of new glass.

What is the difference between factory cullet and foreign cullet?

Factory cullet is glass waste generated inside a glass plant, so its composition is known. Foreign cullet, also called post-consumer cullet, is glass recovered after use, from bottles, jars and other discarded glass, and needs more sorting and processing.

What is flint cullet?

Flint cullet is clear, colourless glass cullet, also called white cullet, kept apart from amber and green for colourless glass production.

Why is cullet sorted by colour?

Colour decides where glass can go next. Clear glass production is very sensitive to coloured glass, so cullet is sorted into clear, amber and green and each goes back into matching production.

Why do glass manufacturers use cullet?

Cullet melts at a lower temperature than virgin raw materials, which reduces furnace energy use and CO2 emissions and cuts the amount of sand, soda ash and limestone needed.

What contamination matters most in cullet?

Ceramics, stone and porcelain, which do not melt; metals such as caps and rings; organic material such as labels and residue; wrong colours; and non-soda-lime glass such as heat-resistant cookware.
