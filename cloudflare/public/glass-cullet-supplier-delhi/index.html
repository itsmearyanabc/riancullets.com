Glass cullet supplier in Delhi NCR

Sorted, processed glass cullet from Tikri Kalan, Delhi, supplied to glass manufacturers across India since 1996.

Rian Cullet has supplied glass cullet from Delhi for 30 years. We collect waste glass, sort it by type and colour, process it into cullet a furnace can accept, and supply it to glass manufacturers in more than 22 states.

Our yard is at Tikri Kalan in West Delhi, on the Rohtak Road corridor at the Delhi–Haryana border. Delhi NCR is one of the largest sources of waste glass in the country, and buying cullet close to where it is generated keeps freight short for plants across North India.

It is a generational business, founded by Ms. Indu Bhatia, and more than 35 companies have sourced cullet from us.

Cullet we supply

Cullet is supplied by source and by colour, because those two things decide where the glass can go next.

  • Factory cullet: clean, known-composition glass generated inside glass plants.
  • Foreign (post-consumer) cullet: glass recovered after use, then sorted and processed.
  • Flint (clear / white) cullet, for colourless glass production.
  • Amber (brown) cullet, for amber bottles and jars.
  • Green cullet, for green bottles.
  • Container glass and flat glass cullet, kept apart.
02

How supply works

  1. Tell us what you need: cullet type, colour, the quantity and the delivery location.
  2. We confirm availability and agree a dispatch schedule with you.
  3. The material is sorted and processed to suit your furnace.
  4. It is dispatched by road to your plant, anywhere in India.
03

Why manufacturers buy cullet

Cullet melts at a lower temperature than the virgin batch of sand, soda ash and limestone it replaces. Adding it lowers furnace energy use, extends refractory life and reduces the CO2 released both by burning fuel and by breaking down carbonates in the raw materials.

For a glass plant, a dependable cullet supplier is therefore a cost lever as well as a sustainability one. The value depends on consistency: the right colour, kept free of ceramics, stone, metal and organic contamination, delivered when the furnace needs it.
